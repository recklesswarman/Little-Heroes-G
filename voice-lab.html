🦖 Rex Voice Lab

Test page for an offline voice chat. Your speech goes to whisper.cpp on the Pi, Rex answers through the normal Rex chat, and Piper reads the answer out loud.

whisper.cpp Piper

The microphone only works on a secure page. Open this page as http://localhost:3000/voice-lab.html on the Pi itself, or through HTTPS. You can still upload a recording or type below.

Keep it short: recordings stop by themselves after 10 seconds.
